# Animation
- Identify the skeleton, the animation blueprint or state machine, and the assets involved before changing anything; do not retarget or re-export unless asked.
- Keep transitions, blend times and notifies data-driven; check root motion, looping and foot-sync on each changed clip.
- Watch compression, curve count and update cost of graphs; note the numbers if they change.
- Verify in play-in-editor or the animation preview and list the clips and states checked.
