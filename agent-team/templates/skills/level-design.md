# Level design
- State the player goal, flow and pacing of the space first; keep sightlines, scale and traversal consistent with the existing levels.
- Respect streaming, world partition and lighting-build rules; list actors and sublevels touched, and avoid changes to levels another change has opened.
- Playtest the golden path and one failure path; check navigation, triggers and checkpoints.
- Keep placeholders obvious and named; leave final art passes to their owners.
